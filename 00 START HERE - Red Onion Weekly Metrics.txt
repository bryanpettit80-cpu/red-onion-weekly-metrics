RED ONION WEEKLY METRICS - QUICK GUIDE
=======================================

BEFORE YOU START
Use one computer at a time. Let Dropbox finish downloading the whole workspace.
Save and close the daily reports and all Red Onion workbooks in Excel.

WEEKLY SNAPSHOT
1. Save the week's original Marketing Vitals .xls or .xlsx daily reports in:
   01 Daily Reports - Drop Here
2. Check Tuesday through Sunday coverage for Richmond and Virginia Beach:
   six business dates and 12 restaurant-days, normally six daily workbooks
   containing both restaurants. Use the Date(s) field inside each report;
   filenames do not establish the business date. Keep only one week in intake.
   Previously accepted reports can supply other dates in that same week.
3. Double-click Run Weekly Snapshot.cmd in this folder.
4. Read the screen result and 02 Finished Reports\LAST RUN STATUS.txt.
   Check the report week before opening the finished workbooks.

READ THE RESULT
waiting_for_inputs      Supply the listed missing reports or finish downloading.
                        Previous reports remain available; missing is not zero.
needs_review            Have the stated conflict or invalid input reviewed.
published               The complete report set was saved locally.
published_with_warnings The report was saved; review the warning. Do not restore
                        inputs or repeat the week just because cleanup is pending.
already_processed       These facts were accepted; reports were not regenerated.
failed_before_publication Resolve the stated problem before retrying.
history_imported        History was saved without reports. The next ordinary run
                        refreshes the complete accepted history.

FINISHED REPORTS AND MANAGEMENT UPDATES
Weekly restaurant reports and Red_Onion_Server_Master.xlsx are in
02 Finished Reports. Start with How to Use; check Management Center readiness.
Edit the supported blue cells in Management Center. Targets, owners, and
Current Actions updates carry forward. Keep tables, columns, and Action IDs
intact. Save and close the master before the next run. Keep exploratory edits
in a separate review copy; different management edits in multiple masters
require a choice. Correct reporting data through source review, not by changing
a generated number. Verified closures require an explicit configuration entry.

KEEP HISTORY AND BACKUPS TOGETHER
Keep all three numbered folders, program, and launcher together. Do not move,
rename, or delete archive contents to resolve a run message. Accepted sources
and recovery history are saved under 03 Archive, including Reporting State.
Inputs leave intake after publication and saved-copy verification; warnings
may leave cleanup pending. Keep the existing archive for the first import.
Back up the whole Red Onion Metrics folder, including the latest edited master
and Reporting State, to an independent location outside the live synced folder.
A program-only download or Dropbox sync alone is not an independent backup.
Before switching PCs, finish the run, close Excel, and let Dropbox finish
uploading here and downloading there. Local success does not prove cloud sync.
See the program's SETUP.md and RECOVERY.md for setup or recovery help.

FOLDER MAP
01 Daily Reports - Drop Here         Current daily reports waiting to run
02 Finished Reports                  Weekly workbooks, master, and run status
03 Archive                           Saved source history and recovery files
Red Onion Weekly Metrics Automation  Program and help; operators do not edit

An empty drop folder is normal. Excel temporary files beginning with ~$ are ignored.
